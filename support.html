Beta testers

If you're testing a pre-release build through Apple's TestFlight, send feedback from there: in the TestFlight app, choose Send Beta Feedback, or take a screenshot while using the app to send feedback. Crash reports also reach us through TestFlight.

Reporting a problem from the app

In the app, go to Settings → Diagnostics & Support → Report a Problem. You'll review exactly what's included before doing anything with it, then tap Email Report to send it straight to us. No account is needed, and you can add to it before you send. You can also copy or share the report instead.

The same screen has Export Diagnostics, which lets you preview every line of a diagnostics file before sharing it.

Keys and personal identifiers in a report are masked automatically before you see it. Your conversations, contacts, location, and saved memories are never included.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. There is no OpenGlasses account or sign-up.

Do I need glasses?

No. Phone-based features and camera fallback let you use OpenGlasses without compatible smart glasses.

Which AI does it use?

You choose. Use a cloud AI provider, connect your own server, or run a compatible model on your iPhone.

How do I delete my data?

Most of what the app keeps lives on your iPhone, so you control it. Conversations, memories, faces, recordings, notes, places, documents and vaults can each be deleted from within the app, and you can set an automatic retention period for conversation history. Deleting the app removes its stored data; see the retention and deletion section of the privacy policy for the details, including Keychain items that can outlive an uninstall.

Does browser streaming send my video to Skunkworks NZ Ltd?

Yes. Browser streaming for Field Assist uses a relay server that you or your organisation run—the app ships with no relay address, and streaming doesn't start until you enter one in Settings. Frames go only to that relay, and from there to the people who open your stream link.
