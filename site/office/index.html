What it is for

One desk for the whole field team.

The office end of Field Assist: the technician's phone does the work on site, and the office is where jobs start and records end up.

Send

Jobs out

Number, site, fault report and the equipment on file, prepared here and sent to the technician's phone. They review it before it is added.

Receive

Reports back

Work records, customer PDFs, clips and parts requests return from the phone and are filed by job and visit.

Keep

Manuals, procedures, vaults

Import manuals, write and approve procedures, and release them to every phone as one checked package.

Learn

Learnings

Short findings from completed jobs, reviewed at the office and shown to the technician on the next similar job.

Phones

Phones you can trust, and let go of.

Pairing

Pair by scan

The office shows a code, the phone scans it, both screens show the same check code, and the office approves.

Trust

Everything signed

Jobs, manuals, reports and receipts carry a signature from the office or the phone. A name or network address is never trusted on its own.

Contact

Kept in touch

A pairing is time-limited and renews while the phone stays in contact. Out of touch for too long, it lapses and the phone is paired again.

Leaving

Remove or revoke

Staff leaving? Remove the phone in good order, or revoke it at once: sharing stops, queued jobs are cancelled, and licensed content on the phone locks.

The office keeps the record: which phone was given what, when, and the signed receipt that came back.

Where your information lives

Your records stay on your own computer.

Avenkin Office runs on a computer the organisation owns. Jobs, manuals and reports are kept on that computer, and phones sync with it directly. We do not host a server that holds an organisation's data, and this website never sees jobs, manuals or reports.

Your office, your computer, your data

No dependence on us.

Storage

On your computer

Jobs, reports, manuals and device records live on the office machine. No vendor server or cloud database holds them.

Sync

Phone to office, directly

Over your Wi-Fi when they share it, otherwise over mobile data through a relay that passes traffic on and keeps nothing.

Availability

Keeps working without us

The only call to us is licence activation. If our service is unreachable, your office keeps working.

Backup

Backup is a copy

The office folder is plain files: copy it with the app closed and keep it with your other backups.

Formats

Open formats

Jobs export as CSV. Reports are PDF plus structured JSON for your own systems.

Scope

One office per organisation

A single office computer holds the record for all of your phones.
