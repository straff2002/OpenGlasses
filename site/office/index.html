Avenkin Office

A desktop app that brings jobs, manuals and devices together for teams in the field.

What it is for

One desk for the whole field team.

This page describes what Avenkin Office is being built to do.

1

Devices

Link the organisation's phones to the office and set the rules they follow.

2

Manuals

Assign the organisation's own manuals and knowledge vaults to the phones that need them.

3

Jobs

Issue a job to a phone and receive the report and session record when the work is done.

Where your information lives

Your records stay on your own computer.

Avenkin Office runs on a computer the organisation owns. Jobs, manuals and reports are kept on that computer, and phones sync with it directly. We do not host a server that holds an organisation's data, and this website never sees jobs, manuals or reports.

How it will be delivered

A download, not a service.
