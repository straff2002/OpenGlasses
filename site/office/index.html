Avenkin Office

A desktop app that brings jobs, manuals and devices together for teams in the field.

In development. Avenkin Office has not been released and there is no download yet. This page describes what it is being built to do.

What it is for

  • Devices. Link the organisation's phones to the office and set the rules they follow.
  • Manuals. Assign the organisation's own manuals and knowledge vaults to the phones that need them.
  • Jobs. Issue a job to a phone and receive the report and session record when the work is done.

Where your information lives

Avenkin Office runs on a computer the organisation owns. Jobs, manuals and reports are kept on that computer, and phones sync with it directly. We do not host a server that holds an organisation's data, and this website never sees jobs, manuals or reports.

How it will be delivered

Avenkin Office is planned as a direct download for macOS and Windows from this site, with signed installers and published checksums. It is licensed per organisation.
