What this version does

In 0.5.0.

Jobs

Jobs out, reports back

Prepare a job with its number, site, fault report and equipment on file, send it to a paired phone, and file the work record and customer PDF that come back.

Manuals

Manuals, procedures and vaults

Import manuals, keep the organisation's own procedures beside them, and release them to the phones that need them as one checked package.

Devices

Pair by scan

The office shows a code, the phone scans it, both show the same check code, and the office approves. Remove a phone in good order or revoke it at once.

Records

Recordings, scenarios, learnings

Session recordings arrive from the phone, scenarios can be sent to devices, and short learnings from completed jobs are kept for the next similar job.

Known limits in this version: Apple silicon Macs only; activation is by a request file and a reply file rather than online; the office's settings cannot be changed after activation; recordings are stored but cannot yet be played back in the app; one office computer per organisation.

Where your information lives

Your records stay on your own computer.

The office folder is plain files on the Mac you install this on. Phones sync with it directly. We do not host a server that holds an organisation's data, and the only call to us is licence activation.

Set up your office.

Download the app, read the guide, and write to us for a licence. If something does not work as the guide says, tell us and we will sort it out.
