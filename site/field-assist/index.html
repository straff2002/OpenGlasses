For the trades

Field Assist

Field Assist, powered by Avenkin, puts procedures and reference knowledge within speaking distance.

Follow guided steps, report a reading, look up a fault, and bring in a remote expert when the job needs another pair of eyes. It runs in the Avenkin app, on the phone alone or hands-free with glasses.

Your own manuals

Teams can add their own manuals and knowledge vaults, so answers come with source references and each session leaves a record that can be exported. The vault guide (PDF) explains how to build one.

Access levels

LevelWhat it includes
SoloBundled vaults, guided procedures, domain calculators, session log, expert escalation
TeamSolo capabilities plus custom vaults and manuals, and audited export
EnterpriseTeam capabilities with separately agreed commercial terms

In the app, Settings → Field Assist shows your access and is where a signed licence is activated. Purchase and trial availability depend on the build and how it was distributed.

For organisations

Avenkin Office is the desktop app for the organisation behind the people in the field: it is being built to hand out jobs and manuals and to collect the records that come back.
