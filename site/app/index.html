Avenkin for iPhone

A private AI assistant on your phone, from your wrist, or hands-free with glasses.

Join the beta on TestFlight Capability guide

What it does

Five things, one assistant.

Ask about what you see, translate a conversation, remember the details, and get things done.

See

See and understand

Read a sign, ask about a piece of equipment, or turn a business card into a contact, with scene descriptions, text recognition and live visual coaching.

Talk

Talk and translate

Ask a quick question or settle into a live voice conversation, translate spoken language and follow along with captions.

Remember

Remember

Save where you parked, keep notes about people and places, and turn captured conversations into meeting notes you can ask about later.

Act

Act

Check your calendar, set a reminder, find directions, control your music or the lights, and connect Home Assistant, Siri Shortcuts and MCP tool servers.

Offline

Keep going offline

With the on-device setup (local AI, speech recognition and voice), the conversation runs offline on your iPhone.

What you need

Start with the phone in your pocket.

1

An iPhone

Running iOS 26 or later. That is enough on its own.

2

An Apple Watch, if you like

The assistant from your wrist, without reaching for the phone.

3

Glasses, if you like

Compatible Meta smart glasses add hands-free camera and audio.

Your assistant, your choice

Buy once. Bring the AI you trust.

No subscription to us—a cloud AI provider you choose bills its own usage. Pick the AI, speech recognition and spoken voice separately.

A cloud model

Use the provider you already have, with your own key.

Your own server

Point Avenkin at a model you run yourself.

On your iPhone

Compatible local models with Apple MLX or llama.cpp, with on-device speech and voice, for a conversation that needs no cloud API key.
