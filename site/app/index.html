The app

Avenkin for iPhone

A private AI assistant on your phone, from your wrist, or hands-free with glasses.

Ask about what you see, translate a conversation, remember the details, and get things done. Buy once, with no subscription to us—a cloud AI provider you choose bills its own usage. With the on-device setup (local AI, speech recognition and voice), the conversation runs offline on your iPhone.

What it does

  • See and understand. Read a sign, ask about a piece of equipment, or turn a business card into a contact, with scene descriptions, text recognition and live visual coaching.
  • Talk and translate. Ask a quick question or settle into a live voice conversation, translate spoken language and follow along with captions.
  • Remember. Save where you parked, keep notes about people and places, and turn captured conversations into meeting notes you can ask about later.
  • Act. Check your calendar, set a reminder, find directions, control your music or the lights, and connect Home Assistant, Siri Shortcuts and MCP tool servers.
  • Choose your AI. Use a cloud model, a model on your own server, or a local model on your iPhone, and pick speech recognition and voice separately.

Read the full overview or the capability guide.

What you need

An iPhone running iOS 26 or later—that is enough on its own. An Apple Watch gives you the assistant from your wrist, and compatible Meta smart glasses add hands-free camera and audio when you want them.

Availability

Avenkin is coming to the App Store. Until then you can join the beta on TestFlight, Apple's app for testing pre-release builds. The app's source is also published, so you can build it from source on a Mac.

Privacy

There is no Avenkin account, and the app has no analytics, advertising or tracking of its own. What it keeps is stored on your iPhone. Read the privacy policy.
