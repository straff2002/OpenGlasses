Avenkin for iPhone

A private AI assistant on your iPhone, through headphones or AirPods, from your wrist, or hands-free with glasses.

What it does

More ways to use your assistant.

Ask about what you see, translate a conversation, remember the details, and get things done.

See

See and understand

Read a sign, ask about a piece of equipment, or read contact details from a business card, with scene descriptions, text recognition and live visual coaching.

Talk

Talk and translate

Ask a quick question or settle into a live voice conversation, translate spoken language and follow along with captions.

Remember

Remember

Save where you parked, keep notes about people and places, and return to saved conversations. The on-device brain connects useful context for later questions.

Memory, the brain and your controls

Act

Act

Check your calendar, set a reminder, find directions, control your music or the lights, and connect Home Assistant, Siri Shortcuts and MCP tool servers.

Offline

Keep going offline

With the on-device setup (local AI, speech recognition and voice), the conversation runs offline on your device.

Accessibility

Read, listen and follow along

Reading Assistant, Blind Assistant, spoken descriptions, captions, VoiceOver support and voice controls.

Features, setup and current limits

Capture

Photos, recordings and sharing

Capture photos and video, keep audio recordings for playback and transcription, or share a view with a configured remote expert connection.

Your services and tools

Connect Home Assistant, Siri Shortcuts or compatible MCP servers, and add custom tools. Available actions depend on each service and its permissions.

What you need

Start with your iPhone.

The standard phone version is also usable on iPad and iPhone Duo. Dedicated versions for those devices are coming soon.

1

An iPhone

Running iOS 26 or later. That is enough on its own.

2

Headphones or AirPods, if you like

Spoken replies through headphones or AirPods connected to your iPhone or iPad.

3

An Apple Watch, if you like

The assistant from your wrist, using the iPhone paired with your Apple Watch.

4

Glasses, if you like

Compatible Meta smart glasses connect through your iPhone to add hands-free camera and audio.

Your assistant, your choice

Bring the AI you trust.

Pick the AI, speech recognition and spoken voice separately.

A cloud model

Use the provider you already have, with your own key.

Your own server

Point Avenkin at a model you run yourself.

On your device

Apple Intelligence on supported devices, or compatible downloaded models running with MLX or llama.cpp. Choose on-device speech and voice for a conversation that needs no cloud API key.

Apple Intelligence uses Apple's on-device Foundation Models framework and needs no API key. It must be enabled and its system model ready. It supports text conversations and a selected set of enabled app tools, prioritising Field Assist actions when that mode is active. Camera understanding still needs a vision-capable model.
