```
A hands-free AI assistant for iPhone, with or without smart glasses. Ask a question out loud and hear the answer through Meta glasses, AirPods or your phone.

WHAT IT DOES
• Looks for you – describe a scene, read signs, labels, documents and gauges through the glasses camera or your iPhone's
• Gets things done by voice – notes, reminders, timers, calendar, messages, directions, web search and smart home
• Captures hands-free – photos, video and live streaming from your glasses
• Catches what you missed – "what did they just say?" replay of the last few minutes, and meeting summaries with decisions and action items

MEMORY
Say "remember that…" and Avenkin keeps it, so you can correct it or have it forgotten whenever you like. Saved conversations let it remember who you are across sessions. You choose how long history is kept, and anything past that is deleted, not hidden.

YOUR CHOICE OF AI
Sign in with your ChatGPT or Claude account – no API key needed (Claude sign-in uses your plan's extra usage). Gemini and other providers work with an API key, or run a model on the iPhone itself. Gemini Live and OpenAI Realtime give you natural back-and-forth voice conversation.

FIELD ASSIST
For service technicians: hands-free guidance grounded in your own manufacturers' manuals. Import manuals into a vault, ask about a fault code or procedure, and get answers that cite the manual and page, with step-by-step procedures you can work through without putting the tools down.
• Van stock by voice – say what you loaded and what you used; it keeps the count with no signal, tells you when you've used the last one, and hands the levels and a top-up list to base at the end of a shift
• Pairs with Avenkin Office so your phone can be managed and connected to the office
Field Assist needs a subscription or an organisation licence.

PRIVACY
No analytics or advertising SDKs. No face recognition. An optional filter blurs bystanders' faces before camera images are sent to an AI or streamed.
```
