Avenkin is a phone/tablet AI assistant. No smart glasses or other external hardware are required.

PHONE-ONLY BUILD
This build is phone-only: the Meta Wearables SDK, glasses registration/callbacks, accessory protocols, glasses background modes, hardware-only capture intents and the silent standby player are excluded. Receipts, remote configuration and licences cannot enable glasses. The glasses-beta TestFlight build is a different binary.

DAILY ALLOWANCE
App Store installs get 15 free minutes of assistant time a day (a turn from send to finished reply; a connected Live session). Standard (com.openglasses.standard_monthly/_annual) or any paid plan or licence removes it. VoiceOver, Accessibility, assistive modes and first aid are never counted or refused. Sandbox installs are unmetered: Settings > Subscription shows the paywall; Advanced & Support > Advanced > Developer > Meter as a store build applies the limit.

GETTING STARTED
No Avenkin account or app sign-in is required. Complete onboarding and choose an AI model in Settings: Apple Intelligence where supported, a downloaded local model, or optional cloud/self-hosted services with the user's own credentials. Tap Type to ask a question, or allow microphone access and use Tap & Talk. Optional permissions can be declined. Phone-camera features work without glasses.

AI SHARING PERMISSION
Before sending content, the app asks approval for the service address and purpose: conversation processing, transcription, spoken replies, live voice/camera or web search. It asks when a key is saved or a model chosen, or at first use; Not now sends nothing. Settings > Devices & Privacy > Cloud AI Sharing lists each configured service with Allow and Withdraw. A new destination or purpose needs separate approval. Withdrawal cancels transfers and blocks later sends. Unapproved background requests refuse, say why, and ask when the app is next opened. On-device engines and system voices need no approval.

MY MANUALS - PERSONAL SUBSCRIPTION
Settings > My Manuals > Upload manual > Choose PDF, Word or Markdown. Selection and preview work before purchase; saving and cited questions need Personal access. Photograph pages also works. Import the fictional Markdown manuals in the review kit, select them, ask about their reference cards, open a citation, then delete one. No manufacturer workshop PDF is bundled. New purchases offer com.openglasses.my_manuals_monthly (one month). Restore purchases restores verified access. Personal does not unlock Field Assist.

FIELD ASSIST
Settings > Field Assist: previews and paywall; new solo purchases offer com.openglasses.field_assist_monthly. Organisation features use signed licence codes or activation keys; no external-purchase link.

MEDICAL AND OTHER FEATURES
Settings > Devices & Privacy > Medical Compliance offers com.openglasses.medical_compliance_monthly and com.openglasses.medical_compliance_annual. It adds clinical privacy/retention controls and export formats, not certification of legal compliance or clinical accuracy. Use fictional records for testing.

The app also includes first-aid coaching, advisory camera triage, medication tools and a clinical persona. AI answers need checking and never replace emergency services or professional judgement. Tool actions keep permission/confirmation checks. Listening has stop controls. Calendar, Reminders, Contacts, Home, Music and Shortcuts use Apple frameworks; tool support varies by model.

REPORTING AND CONNECTIONS
Long-press an AI reply > Report this reply: the user reviews the question, reply, model and version, then emails support (also on support.html).
Providers' own content filters also apply.
ATS exception *.ts.net (plus local networking): plain HTTP in Tailscale's WireGuard tunnel to user's own servers.
Gateways, MCP servers, skill packs and workflows are user-configured (Connections, Tools & Actions). Packs are text/data and MCP tools run on servers the user adds; neither is executable app code.
