# Line values for the start-of-shift check

These are the example line's own limits. A real site writes its own from its product
specifications and its quality plan.

| Check | Unit | Readable range | Spec |
|---|---|---|---|
| Carbonation | volumes CO2 | 0 to 6 | 2.4 to 2.8 |
| Brix | °Bx | 0 to 30 | 10.5 to 11.5 |
| Fill height (from the finish) | mm | 0 to 100 | 55 to 60 |
| Cap removal torque | N·m | 0 to 10 | 1.2 to 2.0 |
