# Models — SLP99UHVK series (Dave Lennox Signature Collection, R-454B units)

Six models. Source: SLP99UHVK Service Manual p.2 (Specifications), Installation Instructions p.2 (Unit Dimensions), p.33 (TABLE 12), p.70 (Configuring Unit Size Codes).

How the nameplate reads: `SLP99UH090XV60CK`. The manuals also write each unit three other ways (without the X, hyphenated with the width letter, or as the short input-blower-width form); every spelling sits in that model's own heading below so any of them looks up. The manuals do not publish a digit-by-digit decoder; what the segments correspond to in the tables is: `070/090/110/135` the input group, `36/48/60` the blower size (add-on cooling range below), `B/C/D` the cabinet width group, `K` the series.

Common to all models: variable-capacity gas valve, variable-speed combustion air inducer and indoor blower (SM p.1). 120 V, 60 Hz, single phase. Direct vent (two-pipe) only, 2 in. PVC intake and exhaust connections, 1/2 in. IPS gas connection, 3/4 in. condensate trap. Factory natural gas; LP/propane needs the changeover kit in service_values.md. Manifold pressure 3.5 in. w.g. natural / 10.0 LP at maximum input and 0.5 / 1.5 at minimum input, all models (SM p.2). High static 0.8 in. w.g. Furnaces cannot be twinned (SM p.3).
