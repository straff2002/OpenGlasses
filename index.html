What it does

Say it. See it. Get it done.

One assistant for what is in front of you, what was said, and what needs doing next.

See

Understands what you are looking at

Read a sign, ask about a piece of equipment, or turn a business card into a contact.

“What does this label say?”

Talk

A live conversation, in your language

Ask a quick question or settle in. Translate spoken language and follow along with captions.

“Translate what she is saying.”

Remember

Keeps the details that matter

Where you parked, notes about people and places, and meeting notes you can ask about later.

“What did we agree on Tuesday?”

Act

Does the next thing for you

Calendar, reminders, directions, music and the lights, plus Home Assistant, Siri Shortcuts and MCP tool servers.

“Turn on the workshop lights.”

Choose

Runs on the AI you pick

A cloud model, a model on your own server, or a local model on your iPhone. Route different requests to different models.

“Use my own server for this.”

Offline

Keeps going without a signal

With the on-device setup—local AI, speech recognition and voice—the conversation runs offline on your iPhone.

“No signal? Carry on.”

Where it lives

One assistant, three ways to reach it.

The phone is all you need. The rest is there when you want your hands free.

1

Your phone

An iPhone running iOS 26 or later is enough on its own, camera included.

2

Your wrist

Start a conversation from your Apple Watch without reaching for the phone.

3

Your glasses

Compatible Meta smart glasses add hands-free camera and audio.

Private by design

No account. No analytics. No one in the middle.

It stays on your iPhone

Conversation history, memories, notes and recordings are stored on your phone.

It goes only where you send it

Data leaves your phone only when a feature needs an outside service, such as the AI provider you picked.

This site keeps the same promise

No cookies, no analytics and nothing loaded from anyone else. Read the privacy policy.
