Private by design

There is no Avenkin account, and the app has no analytics, advertising or tracking of its own. What it keeps is stored on your iPhone. Data leaves your phone only when you use a feature that needs an outside service, such as the AI provider you picked, and it goes only to the services you choose.

This website makes the same promise: no cookies, no analytics and nothing loaded from anyone else. Read the privacy policy.
